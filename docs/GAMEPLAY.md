# How to play

Tagline: **Play for fun. Challenge your brain. Compete with everyone.**

## First session

1. Splash (tap to skip).
2. Play as guest, or create a local account.
3. Home: **PLAY NOW** starts a 60-second rush.
4. After the first match, guests get **Save your score**.

## 60-second rush

- Optional 3-2-1-**GO!** (Settings can skip it).
- Four answer pads. Keyboard **1–4**. **Space** pauses.
- Faster answers pay a speed bonus. Combos shout at x5 / x8 / x12.
- A miss pauses the clock for **Did you know?**
- Difficulty chips during countdown: all / easy / medium / hard.
- Leaving without finishing does not save the run.
- At 10 seconds the arena warns (haptic + countdown sound).

Practice rush is half XP and does not update ranked best, season, or weekly boards.

## Mini-games

- **Reaction** — wait for green, tap. Pause is allowed except on a live GO. HUD shows lifetime best and last hit.
- **Memory** — 4×4 pairs. Pause freezes the stopwatch. Sub-40s unlocks Total Recall.
- **Accuracy** — hit the moving target. Pause freezes clock and motion.

All three confirm before you abandon a run.

## 1v1

Eight shared questions vs CPU (Rookie / Rival / Ace). Keys 1–4. Review is saved. Challenge a rival from Home, Friends, Results, or a leaderboard row.

## Daily loop

- Claim streak loot (7-day table). Day-7 can grant a streak saver (max 2).
- Daily challenge category rotates by weekday.
- Three missions: ranked rush, 8 correct, one mini-game.
- Question of the day: one shot, +20 coins / +25 XP.
- Daily XP goal: 400 XP → Daily Grind.

## Progression (not pay-to-win)

XP → level titles through MASTER. Coins buy looks only. MindArena Plus is a local demo (no ads flag, gold skin, +15% XP). Rewarded-ad preview grants 2× XP for ~20 minutes.

## Boards

Global, country, university, season, weekly. Search + Find me. Weekly best resets Monday.

## Settings worth knowing

Music / SFX + volume, haptics, reduce motion, skip countdown, color-assist pads, pinned mode, CPU skill, graphics quality, arena accent, copy/restore backup (password hash stripped).
