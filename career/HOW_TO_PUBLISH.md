# Step-by-step: paste your LinkedIn profile

Do this on a **computer** (Chrome or Edge). Phone LinkedIn hides many buttons.

Keep **two windows** side by side:

1. This folder in Cursor: `career/LINKEDIN.md` and `career/PROJECTS.md`
2. [linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com) — log in **yourself**

Copy only the text **inside** the gray ``` boxes. Do not copy the word `Name:` or the markdown `#` headings.

After every form, click **Save**.

---

## Step 0 — Open your profile

1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com and sign in.
2. Top right: click your small photo **Me**.
3. Click **View Profile**.

You should see your name, a big cover photo area, and a round profile photo.

---

## Step 1 — Profile photo

1. Move the mouse onto your **round photo**.
2. Click the **camera**.
3. Upload a clear photo of **only you**, plain wall, face large.
4. Crop so your face is in the center → **Save photo**.

Skip if you will do photo later. Do not use a group picture.

---

## Step 2 — Cover banner (optional but looks professional)

1. In Cursor, open `career/portfolio/banner.svg`.
2. Right-click the file → **Reveal in File Explorer**.
3. Double-click it so it opens in Chrome.
4. Press `F11` for full screen if needed. Take a screenshot (`Win + Shift + S`) of the dark banner.
5. On LinkedIn, hover the **wide cover** at the top → click the **camera** → **Upload**.
6. Use the screenshot. Save.

If this is confusing, skip Step 2 and continue. Headline matters more.

## Step 5 — About (the long paragraph)

1. Scroll down. If you see **About**, click the **pencil**.
2. If there is no About: click **Add profile section** (button under your name, near Open to).
3. Click **About** → **Add about**.

4. Open `LINKEDIN.md` → **section 3. About**.
5. Copy **everything inside the code box** (starts with `I am a 3rd-year Computer Science student...` ends with `Lahore, Pakistan`).
6. Paste into the About box.
7. **Save**.

## Step 8 — Five projects (do this 5 times)

1. **Add profile section**.
2. Open **Additional** (or Recommended).
3. Click **Projects** → **Add project**.

If you cannot find Projects: click **Add profile section** and use the search box, type `Projects`.

For **each** project in `PROJECTS.md`:

1. **Project name** → copy the **Name:** line (example: `MindArena — Competitive Mini-Game Platform`).
2. **Start / end dates** → as written in that project (Jan 2026 – Present). Tick currently working if it says Present.
3. **Associated with** → Air University (pick your education).
4. **Description** → copy the big code box under that project. Paste.
5. Skills → add the skills listed under that project.
6. Media / link → skip until you have GitHub.
7. **Save**.
8. Repeat for projects 2, 3, 4, 5.

Order to add:

1. MindArena  
2. AlgoForge  
3. Arena Intel  
4. Nexus Campus  
5. Pulsefolio
